## Interpretation

Cuppet preserved output quality and used fewer workspace-tool calls, but this
five-task Luna-high sample does not show a model-token efficiency win: both
uncached input and total model tokens were higher. The 113 ms median latency
difference is directionally favorable but too small for a strong speed claim.

OpenCode supplied zero cost for every Luna session despite recording tokens,
so monetary cost is not measurable from this provider telemetry. Do not treat
the zeroes as free usage or claim a cost reduction from this run.

This is a five-task read-only navigation benchmark, not evidence for a
product-wide claim. A multi-turn edit suite and provider billing telemetry are
needed for a stronger cost/quality conclusion.
