## Success-conditioned metrics

The all-trial medians above include fast incomplete sessions. Looking only at
trials that passed every acceptance check, OpenCode had 5 successful trials and
Cuppet had 2:

| Metric | OpenCode successful trials | Cuppet successful trials | Cuppet vs OpenCode |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Median latency | 200,233 ms | 230,374 ms | 15.1% higher |
| Median uncached input | 58,334 | 77,596 | 33.0% higher |
| Median total model tokens | 72,189 | 89,739 | 24.3% higher |
| Median cost | $0.287087 | $0.362980 | 26.4% higher |

This distinction matters: Cuppet's lower all-trial cost and latency are partly
driven by three incomplete sessions ending early. Among successful completions,
OpenCode was both more reliable and more efficient on this refactor.
